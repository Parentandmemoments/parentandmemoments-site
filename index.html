Meet Your Author

I once hid in the bathroom for five minutes
and called it self-care.

I'm Chia — working mum, toddler negotiator, and the person who once ran on empty for far too long. I have two little ones: a fierce 4-year-old girl with opinions about everything (including which direction her toast is cut) and a 2-year-old boy who is essentially a golden retriever puppy in a child's body.

I'm not a parenting expert. I'm a mum in the middle of it, learning as I go and sharing everything that's actually helping. If you're tired, overwhelmed, and still showing up every day with cold coffee and good intentions — this space is for you.
